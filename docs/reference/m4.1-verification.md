## Task 1 decision and result: gated realistic layout (scratch, deleted)

User decision 2026-11-03: `open` and `dense` become report-only; add a gated layout `skirmish3v3` with 35 units per player; "we need a way to at least stay close to our goal, otherwise we get a 5 fps slideshow". 50 per player judged a lot. Rest of the proposal accepted (keep 1.67 / 3.3 ms budget on the gated layout; resume with the rebuilt-grid scan at cadence 15).

Layout `skirmish3v3` (scratch prototype, 64x64, two owners): three lanes (y 10 / 30 / 50) with fronts 28 / 40 / 16 cells apart so clashes start at different times; per lane and per owner 35 units (12 tanks, 23 infantry, 5 rows x 7 columns); the first 26 get `attack_move` across their lane, the last 9 stay idle behind (about a quarter). 210 units, 91 alive at the end, 119 deaths, first shot tick 159-163.

| Variant | skirmish3v3 p95 / p99 (ms; budget 1.67 / 3.3) | `combat_work` p95 |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline (no scan), two runs | 0.769 / 0.849 and 0.777 / 0.853 | 1,177 |
| Scan + rebuilt grid, cadence 15, two runs | 0.810 / 0.997 and 0.811 / 1.003 | 434 |

Task 1 gate met: the gated layout has about 2x headroom with the scan. `open`/`dense` with the scan stay over 1.67 (see tables above) and are report-only from here. Rally cost: 20 consecutive `_assign_move` single-unit calls on the 48x32 skirmish map take 1.91-2.03 ms each, a spawn-tick spike reported separately (a rally spawn is one unit at a time).

Rulings (builder; user may veto):
- Report-only is not unbounded: at Task 8 the measured `open`/`dense` p95/p99 become a pinned ceiling (measured + 5%) in `run_combat.gd`, and the benchmark fails above it, so the numbers cannot creep silently. Raising a ceiling needs user approval and a ledger line.
- The gated layout is deliberately lighter than `open`/`dense`; headroom is large, so the ratchet on the stress rows is what catches growth.
- Layout is built in `combat_workload.gd`/`run_combat.gd` at Task 8 (Task 1 stays scratch-only); the scan lands in Task 3 with the rebuilt 8-cell grid in place of `_targets`/`_nearest` sweeping, tie-break unchanged (nearest, then lower ID).
