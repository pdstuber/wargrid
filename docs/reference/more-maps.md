## Converter changes

- **Water height.** Fallen Empire has one water polygon (height 7) over the whole map, with terrain above it. A cell is now wet only when the polygon contains it and the ground height is below the polygon's height. Before, every start was "wet" and the conversion failed (`start not open: seat 1`). Tournament Desert's wet cells fell from 372 to 259. Twilight Flame has no water; `ashfall.json` regenerated byte-identical to `main` until the supply center slot change below.
- **Skipped:** two `TechOilDerrick` on Tournament Desert (no tech buildings in Wargrid).
